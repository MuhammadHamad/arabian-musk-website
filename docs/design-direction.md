# Design direction (v3, 17 Oct 2026)

The owner asked for the site to look like https://aldeenfragrances.com/, a perfume store in
Peshawar. v3 follows its layout and visual language with Arabian Musk's own name, copy, data and
images. Nothing is copied from it: no text, photos or logo.

## What we took from Al Deen

- **Colour and type.** White pages, one amber accent (`#8C4E03`), headings in bold Cormorant
  Garamond, body in Lato, navigation in spaced DM Sans capitals.
- **Frame.** An amber top bar with WhatsApp and address, then a tall white header: logo left,
  centred menu, cart total and bag on the right.
- **Hero.** A full-width photograph with a slanted lower edge, spaced-capital eyebrow, a very
  large serif headline whose last word changes (Perfume, Oud, Musk), and a white "Shop now" button.
- **Home sections, in order.** About (image slider + chevron accordion + outline button), round
  category images, a dark "collection" photo panel beside product cards, a mosaic of amber and
  photo squares, a two-column text section with a photo, an amber price list beside a photo,
  then the footer.
- **Product cards.** Image, category line, bold serif name, "Impression of …", price range, amber
  "Select options" button.
- **Product page.** Slanted banner, image left; "Arabian Musk – Name (Impression of …)", price
  range, a bordered size box, quantity and an amber add button, a bulleted fact list, category.
- **Footer.** Amber block with Address / logo / Info columns, then a white strip of links.
- **Line-drawn flowers** as faint watermarks. Ours is a rose sprig drawn for this site.

## What stays ours

- Rendered bottles in four settings (plaster with a jaali, marble, amber wall on wood, desert
  sand) with each scent's name and number on the label; the jaali arch in the hero.
- The Arabic name المسك العربي beside the logo and the eight-pointed star texture on amber.
- Honest copy: no testimonials (we have none to quote), "impression of" on inspired scents, the
  not-affiliated note, and only facts from the shop's records (40% oil, alcohol-free attar).
